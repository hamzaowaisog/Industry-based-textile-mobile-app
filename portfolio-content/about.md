# About

I build business software that translates messy day-to-day operations into reliable digital workflows.

My strongest work sits at the intersection of product thinking and backend engineering: identity, inventory accuracy, financial traceability, and reporting pipelines that teams can trust. I prefer systems that are auditable, extensible, and grounded in real constraints over flashy architecture for its own sake.

## Engineering Profile

- Backend-first full-stack builder with strong API and data modeling focus
- Comfortable designing secure-by-default services (JWT, role policies, validation, token flows)
- Strong at operational domains: stock movement, pricing logic, client balances, and transaction records
- Able to pair pragmatic frontends with robust service layers for fast end-to-end delivery

## What Stands Out

- I optimize for business correctness first, then polish for developer ergonomics and UX
- I make technical decisions explicit and traceable (why this model, why this policy, why this flow)
- I ship incrementally while keeping architecture open for future scale

## Current Focus

- Domain-driven backend APIs for SMEs
- Reporting surfaces (PDF exports, financial rollups, monthly metrics)
- Better full-stack cohesion between mobile/web clients and backend domain boundaries
